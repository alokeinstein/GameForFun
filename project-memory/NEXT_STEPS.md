## Recommended Future Platform Enhancements (Optional Roadmap)
1. **Additional Game Modes:**
   - Free-For-All (FFA / Solo Deathmatch) where every player competes independently without teams.
   - Capture The Flag (CTF) or Zone Control with physical capturable flags located in team bases.
2. **Persistent Progression & Customization:**
   - Unlockable character cosmetics, tactical hats, and custom weapon skins.
   - Lifetime player stats (career K/D ratio, win rate, favorite weapon, total hours played).
3. **Advanced Bot Combatants:**
   - AI bot soldiers with navmesh pathfinding for solo practice or filling empty team slots in smaller groups.
4. **Spectator Mode & Replays:**
   - Free-fly drone camera for eliminated players or dedicated broadcasters.
